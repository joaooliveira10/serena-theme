# Third-party notices — Serena Icons (logo edition)

The **Serena Icons** file-icon theme (logo edition) redraws some technology logos so that files are
recognisable at a glance. **Serena Icons Minimal** contains no third-party logos and is covered only by
the extension's own MIT licence.

## Trademarks

All product names, logos and brands are property of their respective owners; use is for identification
only and does not imply endorsement. Serena is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of the
owners listed below. The logos are shown only to identify the file types, tools and frameworks they
belong to. Owners who want a mark changed or removed can open an issue in the Serena repository.

Only logos whose licence allows redistribution **with modification** in an MIT-licensed extension, and
whose owner's published guidelines (checked 2026-09-29) do not forbid recolouring or altering them,
are drawn from their logo. Everything else uses original artwork (see `brands.json`, approach
`original-evocative`).
