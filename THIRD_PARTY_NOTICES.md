# Third-party notices — Serena Icons (logo edition)

The **Serena Icons** file-icon theme (logo edition) redraws some technology logos so that files are
recognisable at a glance. **Serena Icons Minimal** contains no third-party logos and is covered only by
the extension's own MIT licence.

## Trademarks

All product names, logos and brands are property of their respective owners; use is for identification
only and does not imply endorsement. Serena is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of the
owners listed below. The logos are shown only to identify the file types, tools and frameworks they
belong to. Owners who want a mark changed or removed can open an issue in the Serena repository.

A logo is drawn from the original only in one of these two cases:

- its licence allows redistribution **with modification** (the licensed groups below); or
- it has no separate licence, and no rule published by its owner that forbids recolouring or
  simplifying the logo was found (the group "No separate licence").

The second case is weaker than permission. Where the owner publishes a brand or trademark page, the
entry links it. Of those pages, these were re-read in October 2026: TypeScript (asks not to change the
shape of the logo; the shape is kept), Tailwind CSS and Svelte (forbid implying endorsement or causing
confusion), Laravel (allows non-commercial community use that refers to Laravel). For the other brands
in that group no usage rule was found or could be read. They are shown only to identify their file
types and are replaced by original artwork at the owner's request.

Logos whose owner forbids altering or recolouring them, or requires permission to use them, are not
used: those file types show original artwork (the Serena Icons Minimal drawings). For that reason the
logos of React, Jupyter, CircleCI, Qt, Django, Bun, Nx, Cypress, Apache HTTP Server and Apache Maven
were replaced by original artwork in version 1.1.0. Everything else uses original artwork too.
